Qafizz Clips

A small publishing tool for short-form video clips.

Qafizz Clips is a personal publishing tool. It edits authorised highlight clips from creators' long-form videos and live streams (as part of creator clipping programs) into vertical short videos with captions, and posts them to the connected TikTok account.

The tool uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. You sign in with TikTok, approve the permissions, and the tool can then upload videos you have prepared to your own account. It never posts anything you have not prepared, and it does not read your messages, followers or other private data.

What it does

  • Signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) to identify the connected account.
  • Uploads finished video files to that account through the Content Posting API, with a caption you write.
  • Spaces posts out over time so they are not all published at once.
