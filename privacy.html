Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What we collect

When you connect TikTok, the service receives from TikTok only what you approve:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens that let the service upload videos to your account (video.publish / video.upload).

We do not collect your password, messages, contacts, followers, location or payment information.

How we use it

Only to upload the videos and captions you have prepared to your own TikTok account, and to check whether each upload succeeded. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored locally on the computer that runs the tool. They are not uploaded to any server we operate. Videos are sent directly to TikTok.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); the stored tokens then stop working and are deleted. To ask for any data to be deleted, open an issue at the link below.

Third parties

Your data is shared only with TikTok, as needed to post your videos, under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
